  • "Delete Sync" removes items in your SYSTM calendar from your intervals.icu calendar, rather than adding/updating them.
  • "Prefer Outdoor" when set to "Yes" will get the outdoor version of the workout, if one is available.

Athlete ID and API Key are obtained from your intervals.icu page and are required fields.

To get your settings from intervals.icu:

  • Log in to intervals.icu
  • Click the "Settings" menu
  • Scroll to the bottom of the page
  • "Developer Settings" on the right has your Athlete ID and your API key
